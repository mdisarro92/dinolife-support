GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for your little prehistoric world.

How long does an egg take to hatch?

An egg normally incubates for about 30 minutes of real time. Use the Warm action when it becomes available to shorten the wait; attentive care can bring hatching down to roughly 15 minutes. Eggs can't eat or sleep.

How do I help my dinosaur grow?

Keep core care needs in good shape and return to check on your companion. Growth follows real time: a well-cared-for dinosaur generally reaches adulthood in about 3–4 days. Some actions have cooldowns, so you don't need to tap constantly.

Can I choose which dinosaur it becomes?

Your quiz determines your egg family: leaf, volcanic, or river. Once hatched, the types of food you feed your baby influence which of the two species in that family it grows into. There are six species to discover.

What happens if I forget to play or my dinosaur gets sick?

Don't worry: dinosaurs never die in DinoLife. Neglected needs may cause temporary illness and slow growth, but gentle care can restore health. The game is designed to be forgiving if life gets busy.

Where is my progress saved? Can I sync it to a new device?

DinoLife stores progress locally on your device. Version 1.0 does not have accounts or built-in cloud sync, so progress does not automatically transfer between devices through DinoLife. Device-level backup and restore options are managed by Apple and are not guaranteed as an in-game transfer method.

How do I turn off music, sounds, or reminders?

Open DinoLife's About & Privacy screen. You can separately toggle music, sound effects, haptics, and gentle reminders. Notification permission can also be changed in iOS Settings. Reminders are optional and designed to avoid 9 PM–8 AM.

How do I reset my game?

In About & Privacy, choose Erase All Data and confirm twice. This removes the current dinosaur, pantry, Sanctuary, Journal, achievements, and local preferences from this device. This action can't be undone within DinoLife.

Is DinoLife free? Does it collect data?

DinoLife version 1.0 is free with no advertising, purchases, accounts, analytics, or tracking. Gameplay progress stays on your device. Read the privacy policy for details, including what happens if you voluntarily email support.
