YOUR LITTLE WORLD, YOUR DATA

Privacy policy.

DinoLife is built to be a gentle, offline-first dinosaur care experience. We believe your game progress belongs to you.

Effective date: October 10, 2026 · Applies to DinoLife for iPhone and iPad, version 1.0, and this support website.

🔒
The short version

The DinoLife app does not collect personal data from your gameplay. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, in-app purchases, or third-party tracking SDKs. Your progress is stored on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

DinoLife stores its gameplay state and preferences locally on your device, including your dinosaur and its needs, egg progress, food pantry, evolution history, bonds, Sanctuary, Journal discoveries, achievements, and settings. This information is used to make the game work. It is not transmitted to the developer by the app.

2. Accounts, analytics, ads, and sharing

The app does not require an account and does not include developer-operated cloud synchronization, analytics, advertisements, purchases, or third-party tracking software development kits. The developer does not sell or share your game data with advertisers.

3. Optional local notifications

If you give permission, DinoLife can schedule local reminders about care, eggs, and naps. You may turn these reminders off in the app or through iOS Settings. No remote notification service is required for these reminders.

4. Removing game data

Open About & Privacy → Erase All Data in DinoLife and confirm to remove its local save and preferences from this device. Deleting the app also removes its active local app data. Copies that may exist in device backups are managed by Apple's device backup and restore systems.

5. Contacting support

If you voluntarily email m.disarro92@gmail.com, the developer will receive your email address and the information you include. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot issues, not for advertising. Your message also passes through the email providers involved. Avoid including passwords, private screenshots, or other sensitive information. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing the same address.

6. This support website

These pages use no custom analytics, advertising, cookies, embedded trackers, or web forms. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard connection and server information under GitHub's privacy statement. Following the email contact link opens your email application.

7. Children and families

DinoLife is designed for a general family audience. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults through gameplay. If you are helping a child contact support, please avoid including identifying or sensitive personal details.

8. Updates to this policy

If DinoLife's features or data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date. Any new collection or sharing practices will be described here and in applicable App Store disclosures.

9. Questions

For privacy questions or requests about support emails, contact m.disarro92@gmail.com.
